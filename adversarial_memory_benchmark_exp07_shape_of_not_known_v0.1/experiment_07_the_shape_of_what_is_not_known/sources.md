# Sources

This story is fully synthetic. No real Antarctic station, program, incident, or
person is being described or asserted as factual.

Source types inside this corpus are fictional benchmark artifacts:

- `sensor_export` — automated SCADA/instrument/badge-reader exports
- `logbook_entry` — a crew member's own dated log or notebook entry
- `roll_call_record` — the twice-daily attendance log
- `radio_transcript` — station-side log of a scheduled Field Camp Delta contact window
- `incident_report` — a formal filed incident report (e.g. IR-2031-07)
- `maintenance_log` — dated equipment service/inspection records
- `chat_message` — informal intercom/chat messages between crew
- `handover_note` — shift-change or season-file handover notes
- `email_status_record` — satellite relay send/receive status (not content)
- `summary_report` — season-end or periodic summary reports
- `facilities_log` — station engineering/physical-constraint records
- `personal_diary_reference` — a private journal entry, only present in the corpus
  on the one occasion (Nils Berger, Day 65) it was read aloud and transcribed into
  the shared station record; all other personal diaries/notebooks mentioned in the
  corpus remain private and are never transcribed

## Deliberately absent source types

Several sources are referenced by memories in this corpus but their **contents**
are intentionally never included, because their absence is the object of study:

- The sealed personnel memo (Day 56) — content withheld by design, permanently
- Corrupted email bodies (6 incidents this season, including Day 27 and Day 75)
- Diego Fuentes's personal notebook
- The Weather Mast's raw high-frequency sensor archive (destroyed Day 96)
- Any pre-shipment inspection certificate for an unflagged ice-core sample (never
  generated in the first place — distinct from the above, which exist but are
  unavailable)

Do not treat the existence of a reference to one of these sources as license to
infer, support, or contradict its contents. See `story_bible.md`'s Observability
Model and `missingness_map.json` for the authoritative accessibility status of
each.
