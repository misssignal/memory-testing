# Stage 1 — Narrative Selection

## Experiment 01: Temporal Traps
Candidates considered:
1. Remote observatory modernization — strong event/observation/ingestion separation, scoped pilots, site rename, delayed forensic evidence.
2. Hospital lab middleware migration — excellent decision-state traps, but risks accidental safety-domain assumptions.
3. Racing team telemetry redesign — strong temporary exceptions and terminology churn, but fewer natural late-arriving documentary discoveries.

Selected: **Northstar Array remote observatory modernization**.

## Experiment 02: False Consensus
Candidates considered:
1. Music festival site planning — naturally produces permits, decks, training, chats, vendor packets, rumors, and stale operating documents.
2. Startup incident response — excellent citation cascades, but many artifacts would have similar source types.
3. Archaeological expedition provenance dispute — strong rumor/authority structure, but weaker exact-duplicate and institutional-template mechanisms.

Selected: **Nightglass Festival site planning**.
