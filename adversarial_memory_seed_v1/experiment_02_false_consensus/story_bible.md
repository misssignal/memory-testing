# Story Bible — Experiment 02: Nightglass Festival

## Premise
Nightglass is a three-day arts and music festival moving to the former Harborworks shipyard.
A planning spreadsheet accidentally imports an obsolete crowd-capacity figure of 18,000 for the South Yard.
That number is copied into tickets, decks, training notes, vendor plans, and generated summaries until it looks like institutional consensus.
The controlling fire permit actually caps the South Yard at 12,400.

A separate control arc involves a low-water-pressure problem verified independently by five different observations.
Another arc tracks a vendor gate assignment that was once correct, became stale, and remains overrepresented in old documentation.

## Core entities
- Nightglass Festival LLC.
- Harborworks Shipyard, venue.
- South Yard, primary stage field.
- Gate C / Gate F, vendor ingress points.
- Rowan Pierce, festival producer.
- Maya Chen, site operations director.
- Jules Carter, safety coordinator.
- Omar Velasquez, fire marshal liaison.
- Blue Lantern Security, contractor.
- Harborworks Utilities, venue utility team.

## Major truth anchors
- South Yard occupancy limit is 12,400 under the final controlling fire permit.
- The 18,000 figure originates from one obsolete 2024 concept-plan spreadsheet cell and is not an independent observation no matter how many times it is repeated.
- Multiple downstream artifacts copy or paraphrase that same root.
- Five independent observations on 2026-08-11 support the claim that water pressure at the east hydrant loop was abnormally low.
- Gate C was the vendor ingress gate through 2026-07-30; Gate F became the current vendor ingress gate on 2026-07-31.
- Old Gate C material remains much more numerous than the few current Gate F records.
